---
title: "Paper accepted at ICELIE 2026: AI-based Pipeline for Handwritten Diagram Digitalization"
date: 2026-08-29
---

Our paper, *"AI-based Pipeline for Handwritten Diagram
Digitalization"*, has been accepted to [ICELIE 2026](https://icelie2026.ieee-ies.org).

The work was developed with my colleagues [Nicola Ricciardi](https://nicolaricciardi.eu) and [Filippo Garagnani](https://github.com/FGaragnani), and proposes a system for analyzing different types of handwritten diagrams and converting them into well-rendered images through a textual syntax.

Source code is available on GitHub: [diagram-framework/draft](https://github.com/diagram-framework/draft).
