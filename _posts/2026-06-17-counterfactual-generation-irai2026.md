---
title: "Paper accepted at IRAI 2026: Counterfactual Generation for Industrial Time-Series Prognostics"
date: 2026-06-17
---

Our paper, *"Counterfactual Generation for Representation Learning in Industrial Time-Series Prognostics,"* has been accepted to [IRAI 2026](https://irai2026.ieee-ies.org).

The work was developed with my colleague [Nicola Ricciardi](https://nicolaricciardi.eu), and explores how counterfactual generation can shape better representations for prognostics on industrial time-series data — helping models reason about "what if" scenarios in degradation and failure prediction.

Source code is available on GitHub: [cigarsai/counterfactual-generation](https://github.com/cigarsai/counterfactual-generation).
